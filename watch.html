Student Collaboration — Movado Group

HUGO Three-Hand
Watch Design

A three-hand analog watch for HUGO, aimed at a younger, unisex audience — taken from concept to final rendered pieces with Movado's design team.

Movado Group Sep – Nov 2025 SolidWorks KeyShot Adobe Illustrator

The Brief

Appeal to a younger generation, and work equally well on any wrist. I chose a classic three-hand movement — simple and versatile — so proportion, case detailing, and finish carry the personality instead of a busy dial. Regular reviews with Movado's designers and executives shaped each round of refinement.

Recognition

4th Place — Movado Watch Design Competition, among peers.

Spec
Movement: VX32E
Case: 39mm × 9.99mm
Dial opening: 33mm
Research & Ideation

Moodboard & Sketching

A moodboard of consumer electronics, chronographs, and street style set the younger, unisex tone. From there I sketched six case, lug, and crown configurations.

Phase 3

Concept Renders

Three directions went to review — same case architecture, different dial language and color story.

Concept 01

White case, violet gradient bezel — the radar-ring dial at its most graphic.

Concept 02

Matte black case, red-to-pink bezel, and a repeating HUGO-monogram dial texture.

Concept 03

Navy case, rose-gold bezel, and a quieter dual-ring dial — the most understated.

Final Design

Black & Red

The chosen design: a black case with a color-blocked red bezel, radar-ring dial, and a HUGO-patterned strap underside.

Assembly animation — KeyShot

Four Colorways

Colorway Boards

Drawn in Adobe Illustrator on top of the finished design — the same watch, four directions for color. Each board carries the front and side at 100% and 200% alongside the case and strap specs, so a color could be judged at the size it would actually be worn.

All four colorways — Adobe Illustrator

Colorway A — Navy on navy, rose-gold bezel

Colorway B — White strap, purple bezel and dial

Colorway C — Black and red — the final

Colorway D — White strap, warm gold dial
